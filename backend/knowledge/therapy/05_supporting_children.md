# Supporting Children After Domestic Violence

## What children need
Predictable routines, honest age-appropriate reassurance (“the fighting is not your fault”), and a calm adult presence.

## What to avoid
Do not ask children to spy, carry messages, or choose sides. Do not share graphic details.

## Practical steps
- Keep bedtime / school routines if possible
- Allow questions; answer briefly and truthfully
- Seek child counsellor / school counsellor when available
- Document any threats or harm to children for legal advocates

## Your wellbeing
Caring for yourself (food, rest, support) helps you care for them. Asking for NGO or relative help with childcare is strength, not failure.
