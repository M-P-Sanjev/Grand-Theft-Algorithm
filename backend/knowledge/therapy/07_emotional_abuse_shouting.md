# After Shouting and Emotional Abuse

This is supportive first-aid, not therapy.

## Your reaction is valid
Feeling small, shaky, angry, numb, or “bad” after being shouted at is a common trauma response. It does not mean you caused the abuse.

## Right now (2 minutes)
1. Sit or stand with both feet on the floor.
2. Breathe in for 4 counts, out for 6 — three times.
3. Softly name the fact: “Being yelled at hurts. I did not deserve that.”
4. Look around and name 3 objects you can see (grounding).

## What helps next
Message one safe person, or call women helpline **1091** if you feel trapped. Delay big decisions until your body settles. If the person is still nearby and unsafe, leave the room or call **112**.

## Boundaries
You can ask for practical help without explaining everything. Shame often follows emotional abuse — sharing with a counsellor or NGO reduces isolation.
