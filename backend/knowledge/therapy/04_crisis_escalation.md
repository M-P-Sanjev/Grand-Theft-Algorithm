# Crisis Escalation — Self-Harm and Immediate Danger

## If you want to hurt yourself
Please reach a person and emergency help now:
- Call **112** or a local crisis / women helpline (**1091**)
- Go to the nearest hospital emergency
- Stay with someone if you can; remove means of harm if safe

You deserve urgent care. Feelings can peak and then ease with support.

## If the abuser is violent right now
- Prioritize escape over belongings
- Yell for neighbours; call 112
- Do not try to “calm” someone with a weapon — leave

## After the crisis
Medical care, a safe place to sleep, and a gentle check-in with a counsellor or helpline are wise next steps. You are not a burden for asking.
