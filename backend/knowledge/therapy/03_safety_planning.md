# Personal Safety Planning

## Before a crisis
- Pack a small go-bag (ID, medicines, cash, charger, keys, child’s items) with a trusted person if needed
- Memorize helpline numbers: 112, 1091
- Identify exits and a neighbour code word
- Keep important documents in a safe place or cloud account the abuser cannot open

## During escalation
- Move to a room with an exit; avoid kitchens/bathrooms with hard surfaces if possible
- Call for help early; put the phone on speaker if needed
- If you must leave, take children if safe to do so

## After leaving
- Change passwords from a safe device
- Tell school/workplace only what is needed for safety
- Consider a protection order with legal support

Safety plans are personal — adapt to your home and risk level with an NGO advocate when possible.
