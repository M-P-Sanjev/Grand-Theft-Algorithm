# Grounding and Breathing When Overwhelmed

## 5-4-3-2-1 grounding
Name: 5 things you see, 4 you can touch, 3 you hear, 2 you smell, 1 you taste or a positive memory. Speak slowly.

## Box / physiological sigh breathing
- Inhale 4 counts, hold 2, exhale 6 — repeat 3–5 times
- Or: double inhale through nose, long exhale through mouth once

## Body reset
Press feet into the floor. Hold a cold glass or wash face with cool water. Place a hand on chest and say: “I am doing what I can to stay safe.”

## If flashbacks come
Remind yourself of the present date and place. Look around the room. Abuse is not your fault.
