# Boundaries, Shame, and Continuing Support

## Shame and self-blame
Abuse thrives on isolation and shame. Wanting to stay, leave, return, or feel confused are common trauma responses — not proof you “chose” harm.

## Boundaries with others
You may share only what you want. It is okay to say: “I need practical help, not advice right now.”

## Ongoing care
Psychological first aid is a start. Licensed counselling, support groups, and trauma-informed therapy help many survivors over time. If cost is a barrier, ask DLSA / NGO about free or low-cost counselling.

## Digital boundaries
Block or mute when safe. Preserve threatening messages for legal use before deleting if a lawyer advises it.

Remember: this assistant is not a replacement for a human therapist or emergency services.
