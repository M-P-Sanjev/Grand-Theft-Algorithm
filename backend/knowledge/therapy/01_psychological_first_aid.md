# Psychological First Aid for Survivors

This is supportive information, not therapy or a diagnosis. If you are in danger, contact emergency services (112 / 1091).

## Core principles
- Safety first
- Calm presence
- Practical help over analysis
- Respect the survivor’s pace and choices
- Confidentiality within safety limits

## What helps in the first hours
- Move to a place where you can be heard by others if safe
- Drink water, sit, unclench jaw and shoulders
- Name one trustworthy person or helpline to contact
- Delay big life decisions until you feel safer

## What not to do
- Do not blame yourself for the abuse
- Do not argue safety plans with the abuser
- Do not force yourself to “forgive” on someone else’s timeline
