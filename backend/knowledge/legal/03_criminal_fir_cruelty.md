# Criminal Remedies — Cruelty, Assault, and FIR

General information for India; laws continue to evolve (IPC / Bharatiya Nyaya Sanhita).

## Cruelty by husband or relatives
Conduct that drives a woman to suicide or causes grave injury / harassment for dowry may attract cruelty provisions historically under IPC 498A and related BNS provisions. This is a **cognizable** offence that police can investigate after an FIR.

## Physical / sexual assault
Assault, grievous hurt, sexual offences, and stalking have separate criminal provisions. Medical examination (MLC) soon after violence strengthens the case.

## FIR vs DV petition
- **FIR**: starts a criminal investigation; go to the local police station or online portals where available; you can approach a senior officer / Magistrate if police refuse.
- **DV Act petition**: civil protective remedies (orders, residence, maintenance) via Magistrate / Protection Officer.

Both can proceed together. Choose what feels safest; an NGO or legal aid clinic can help sequence steps.

## Zero FIR
You may lodge an FIR at any police station; it should be transferred to the correct jurisdiction later.
