# Evidence Safety and Digital Proof

## Safety first
Never confront the abuser about evidence. Do not store sensitive files where the abuser can easily find them. Prefer cloud accounts the abuser cannot access, or leave copies with a trusted person / lawyer.

## Useful evidence
- Dated photos of injuries or damaged property
- Hospital / MLC records
- Screenshots of threats (keep original devices if possible)
- Call logs, emails, bank statements showing control of money
- Names of neighbours / relatives who witnessed incidents

## Chain of custody mindset
Note date, time, place, and what happened in a private diary. Avoid editing original media; export copies.

## If phone is monitored
Use a friend’s phone or a safe computer at an NGO / cyber cafe carefully. Change passwords from a safe device later.
