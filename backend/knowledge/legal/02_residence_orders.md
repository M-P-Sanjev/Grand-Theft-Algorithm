# Residence Orders and Shared Household (India DV Act)

General information only — consult a lawyer for your facts.

## Residence order
Under the DV Act, a Magistrate may grant a residence order so that the survivor is not thrown out of the shared household, or so she can live in a part of it safely. The court may also restrain the abuser from disturbing possession or selling the home.

## Shared household
“Shared household” can include a home where the woman lived in a domestic relationship, even if title is not in her name. Courts look at facts of residence and relationship.

## Practical tips
- Do not leave belongings behind if leaving is unsafe; prioritize personal safety over property.
- Ask the Protection Officer / NGO about temporary shelter and travel support.
- Keep copies of rent receipts, utility bills, or photos showing you lived there.

## Parallel options
If locked out, also consider police assistance for illegal eviction and urgent interim applications through counsel or legal aid.
