# Child Harm and Reporting (India)

General information only — not legal advice. If a child is in danger, call **112** immediately.

## When a child is hit or harmed
Physical harm to a child can involve criminal offences (assault / cruelty) as well as child-protection pathways. Prioritize getting the child to safety and medical care.

## What to do first
1. Move the child away from the abuser if it is safe to do so.
2. Call **112** or women helpline **1091** and state that a child was assaulted.
3. Seek hospital care; ask for an MLC (medico-legal) record if injured.
4. Ask police / NGO for a woman officer or child-friendly desk where available.

## Parallel civil options
A survivor parent can also seek protection under the Domestic Violence Act (protection / residence orders) through a Protection Officer or Magistrate while criminal reporting proceeds.

## Evidence
Photos of injuries, medical notes, witness names, and a dated timeline help — only collect what is safe. Do not confront the abuser about evidence.

## Support
Service-provider NGOs can accompany you to the police station and help with shelter / counselling for the child.
