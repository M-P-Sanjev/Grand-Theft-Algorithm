# Maintenance, Custody Notes, and Workplace Support

General information only — custody is highly fact-specific.

## Monetary relief / maintenance
DV Act and other family laws may allow interim maintenance, medical expenses, and compensation for losses caused by domestic violence. Bring proof of income, expenses, and children’s needs if safe.

## Children
Courts decide custody and visitation based on the child’s welfare. Document any risk to children. Do not use children as messengers with the abuser.

## Workplace
If violence affects work, some workplaces have POSH policies for sexual harassment at work (different from domestic violence). You may still request flexible leave through HR if safe. Do not email workplace accounts the abuser can access.

## Next step checklist
1. Immediate safety (112 / 1091)
2. Medical care if injured
3. Protection Officer / NGO
4. Decide FIR and/or DV petition with counsel
5. Secure documents and a safe place to stay
