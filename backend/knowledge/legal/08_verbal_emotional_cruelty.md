# Verbal and Emotional Cruelty (India)

General information only.

## Verbal abuse still matters
Repeated shouting, humiliation, threats, isolation, and controlling language can amount to domestic violence under the DV Act even without fresh physical injuries. Courts look at the pattern and impact on the survivor’s wellbeing.

## Legal pathways
- **DV Act petition**: protection order, counselling directions, monetary relief via Magistrate / Protection Officer / NGO — FIR not always required first.
- **Criminal options**: serious threats, stalking, or cruelty patterns may support police complaints; ask an advocate which sections fit your facts.

## Practical steps
Write a private dated log of incidents (words used, date, who was present). Save threatening messages only if your phone is safe. Contact **1091** or an NGO to discuss protection orders and safety planning.

## Children hearing abuse
Exposure to domestic verbal violence can harm children. Mention this when seeking protection or counselling support.
