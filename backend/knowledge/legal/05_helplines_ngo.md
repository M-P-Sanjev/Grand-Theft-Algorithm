# Helplines, Legal Aid, and NGO Pathways (India)

## Emergency numbers
- **112** — national emergency
- **1091** — women helpline (many states)
- **181** — women helpline in several states
- Local police control room / women’s cell

## Legal aid
State Legal Services Authorities (DLSA / SLSA) can appoint free legal aid advocates for eligible survivors. Ask the Protection Officer or court legal aid desk.

## NGO / shelter
Service providers registered under the DV Act can help with counselling, shelter referrals, filing applications, and accompanying you to police or court.

## What to say when calling
State: you need urgent safety help / domestic violence support; your city; whether children are with you; whether the abuser is nearby. Ask for a woman officer if that feels safer.

## Disclaimer
Helpline availability varies by state. If one number fails, try 112 and ask for women’s cell / PCR.
